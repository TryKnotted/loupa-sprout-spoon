Questions tired parents ask us

My child is extremely picky. Will this really help? +

Yes, because the tasks start incredibly small. Instead of asking for a bite, day one might just be touching a food with one finger. That tiny start is exactly what cautious eaters need.

Isn't 14 days too short to change picky eating? +

The goal isn't to fix everything in two weeks. It's to get your child voluntarily touching, smelling, and tasting new foods, and to replace mealtime battles with a calm routine you can keep using.

Do I have to cook special foods every day? +

No. You can use foods you already have at home. The challenge is about how you introduce foods, not about complicated recipes or extra shopping.

What ages is this designed for? +

It's built for children aged 2 to 7 who stick to a short list of safe foods. The language and tasks are tailored to how toddlers and young kids explore new things.

What if we miss a day? +

That's completely fine. There's no deadline and no pressure. Just pick up the next day where you left off; the small steps still work even if your schedule gets messy.

How do I get the challenge after buying? +

It's a digital challenge book delivered straight to your inbox, so you can open it and start day one at your very next meal.
